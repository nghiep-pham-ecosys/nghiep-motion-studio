# nghiep-motion-studio: rules for Claude Code

Public repository. Read `docs/spec.md` first.

- No paid API or paid service. If one is needed, state the estimated monthly cost and stop for Nghiep's yes.
- Nothing is published from this repo. Videos reach YouTube only after Nghiep reviews them (via lab-to-prod).
- Characters and art are original. Never copy characters, images or music from other channels.
- No material from any employer or client of the author. No personal data.
- Rendered output (mp4, wav, frames) stays out of git; CI fails on tracked files over 2 MB.
- Videos made with AI tools are labelled "made with AI" where they are published.
- Commits and PR titles: `feat|fix|chore|refactor|docs|test|ci: description`, body `Refs nghiep-motion-studio#<n>`.
